# Economic Brain design

The Economic Brain is a deterministic calculator and recommender. It cannot
authorize discounts, messaging, cancellation, confirmation or returns.

## Measures

Revenue, product cost, outbound shipping, return, second attempt, agency
pickup, discount and support costs; gross and realised margin; revenue at
risk; expected recovery value; and final realised value.

Every result includes currency, calculation version, contributing source ids,
freshness, completeness, assumptions and confidence. Unknown cost components
remain unknown and lower confidence.

Future analysis compares incident cost, policy margin recovery, discount
profitability, product realised margin, campaign return burden, carrier margin
impact and human-review economic outcomes. Recommendations use versioned rules
and simulation only.
