# Shadow eligibility

C0 defines but does not activate Shadow Mode. Eligibility requires exact/verified identity, critical sources, approved data quality, complete timeline, versioned policy, reproducible timer, deterministic replay, stable decision, blocked actions and an available comparison.

Initial candidate metrics are exact/verified identity rate, comparable-order rate, replay reproducibility, critical-policy parity, timer parity, unexpected-difference rate, connector availability and freshness compliance. Production thresholds are intentionally unset; they require later real-data evidence and owner approval.
