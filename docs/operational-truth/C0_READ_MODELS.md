# C0 read models

Local-only read models exist for truth snapshot, connector health, data quality, reconciliation, parity, replay, migration readiness and operational truth summary.

The aggregate company snapshot reports health, quality, identity counts, comparable/non-comparable orders, reproducible/failed replays, parity by module, discrepancies, risks and readiness. It is not the Company Digital Twin. All models reject direct PII/secret field names and carry zero-action counters.
