# Canonical incident process

The shadow processor preserves the authorized sequence: authenticated event,
deduplication, full issue, actionability, classification, GLS context, allowed
options, current Chatby evidence, policy, feasibility, option validation, Risk
and QA, then simulation or human review.

`MANAGING_WITH_CLIENT` is never proposed merely because contact is planned.
Internal waiting states are used while Dropea remains `PENDING`.

Every proposed resolution must be present in `allowed_resolution_options`.
Unknown identity, stale or contradictory Chatby evidence, unsupported carrier,
infeasible dates and unverified GLS runtime behavior block the proposal.

The 48-hour customer-response timer and 72-hour unknown-review timer are
independent and versioned. There is no new 36-hour timer.

The discount workflow can create `OFFER_PREPARED` and a deterministic
`EMAIL_PREPARED` draft. It cannot send the offer or email, change COD, apply a
discount, resolve an issue or make a retry ready. The discount is strictly
5 EUR and is never greater than 5 EUR.
